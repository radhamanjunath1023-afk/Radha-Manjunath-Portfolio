Moodies Customer Portal Redesign Project

Project Management Case Study

Moodies is a company that designs color-changing shoes based on your mood. As customer inquiries increased, leadership initiated a project to improve the customer support experience.
The project focused on redesigning the customer service website and introducing a chatbot to improve response times and reduce the support workload.

Tools
Jira, WBS, Gantt, RACI, RAID

Skills Demonstrated

Project Planning Jira Scheduling WBS Gantt RACI RAID Risk Management Stakeholder Communication Status Reporting Agile/Scrum

Project Initiation

Business Need

Increasing customer inquiries were adding workload for the support team.

Goal

Redesign the customer service website and introduce a chatbot to improve support efficiency.

Scope

  • Website redesign
  • Support content
  • Chatbot
  • Testing & QA
  • Team enablement

Success Measures

  • Customer satisfaction +20%
  • Rep inquiry time -40%
  • Social inquiries -80%
  • Resolution time 4 → 2 days
