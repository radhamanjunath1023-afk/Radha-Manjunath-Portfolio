Moodies Customer Portal Redesign

Project Management Case Study

A project management case study focused on revamping the Moodies customer service portal and chatbot experience to improve customer access to information and support.

Project Type
Customer Portal Redesign
Role
Project Coordinator / Project Management
Methodology
Agile
Tools
Jira, WBS, Gantt, RACI, RAID

Project Objective

The project focused on improving the Moodies customer service experience through a redesigned customer portal and chatbot. The project plan addressed scope, stakeholders, schedule, budget, risks, and communication throughout the project lifecycle.

Project Initiation

Defined the project purpose, objectives, scope, deliverables, assumptions, constraints, and success criteria.

Project Charter

Established the foundation for the project by defining the business need, objectives, scope, stakeholders, and high-level deliverables.

Key Elements

  • Business need and problem statement
  • Project objectives
  • Scope and deliverables
  • Success criteria
  • Assumptions and constraints

Stakeholder Management

Identified project stakeholders and developed an approach for understanding their responsibilities, expectations, communication needs, and level of involvement.

RACI & Stakeholder Analysis

Used stakeholder analysis and a RACI framework to clarify roles, responsibilities, accountability, and communication needs across the project team.

Key Activities

  • Stakeholder identification
  • Stakeholder analysis
  • RACI development
  • Communication planning
  • Expectation management

Project Plan

Developed the project structure and timeline to organize activities, dependencies, milestones, and deliverables.

WBS & Gantt Schedule

Broke the project into manageable work packages and activities, then organized them into a project timeline with milestones and dependencies.

Planning Activities

  • Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
  • Project schedule
  • Milestone planning
  • Task dependencies
  • Jira work-item tracking

Project Budget

Developed a project budget based on estimated resources, activities, and project requirements.

Budget Planning

Documented estimated project costs and budget assumptions to support financial planning and project monitoring.

Key Elements

  • Resource estimates
  • Cost assumptions
  • Budget allocation
  • Budget monitoring approach

Risk Management

Identified potential project risks and developed response strategies to reduce their potential impact on project objectives.

RAID / Risk Register

Documented risks, impacts, probability, response strategies, and ownership to support ongoing project monitoring.

Risk Management Activities

  • Risk identification
  • Probability and impact assessment
  • Risk mitigation
  • Contingency planning
  • Risk monitoring

Project Reporting

Developed concise project status updates to communicate project health, progress, risks, and upcoming activities to stakeholders.

Project Status Report

Summarized project health, key insights, recommendations, action items, risks, and upcoming activities for stakeholder review.

Reporting Elements

  • Project health
  • Progress updates
  • Risks and issues
  • Key insights
  • Recommendations
  • Action items
